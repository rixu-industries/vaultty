# Privacy policy

Vaultty sends one anonymous message when it starts, and once a day while it stays open, so that
Rixu Industries can count how many installations are in use and in which countries.

## What is sent

- A random identifier created for this installation. It is not derived from your computer, your
  account or your vaults, and it is deleted when you turn this off.
- The Vaultty version, the operating system and the processor architecture.

Like any request on the internet, the message arrives from your IP address. The server works out
the country, region and city from it and does not store the address.

## What is never sent

Nothing about you, your hosts, your credentials or your vaults: no addresses, user names,
passwords, keys, vault names or contents, commands or files. Nothing about what you do in the app
either: there are no events for connections, transfers or clicks.

## Where it goes

To an [Umami](https://umami.is) instance run by Rixu Industries at `analytics.bravinta.com`. The
data is used only for these counts and is not shared with or sold to anyone.

## How to turn it off

- On Windows, clear "Send anonymous usage statistics" in the installer.
- At any time, in Settings > General > "Send anonymous usage statistics".

## Other connections

Vaultty asks GitHub whether a newer version exists and downloads it from there when you choose to
update. No identifier is sent. Every other connection is one you set up: your hosts, tunnels and
proxies.
